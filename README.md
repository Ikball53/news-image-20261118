# The World in One Page — 18 November 2026

A front-page-style image summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026's world
news in a single frame.

## The stories

1. **Trump in Manila** — US President Donald Trump arrives in the
   Philippines for Quad talks ahead of his China visit.
2. **Crimea bridge hit** — Ukraine strikes the Crimean bridge for a
   second consecutive day as Russia pounds bridges in Kyiv.
3. **Iran hits US radar in Bahrain** — Iran attacks a US military radar
   system in Bahrain with drones; the US strikes back at Qeshm Island.
4. **Cyclone Akvan** — the storm over the Bay of Bengal is expected to
   intensify into a severe cyclonic storm.
5. **Dhoni retires** — MS Dhoni ends his IPL playing career and becomes
   Chennai Super Kings mentor.
6. **Canada–China deal** — Canada and China extend trade cooperation as
   Xi Jinping meets Mark Carney ahead of the APEC summit.

Also noted: Britain sanctions Israeli settlement firms; Baidu's chief
warns of an AI bubble.

## How it was made

The artwork is an original editorial illustration composed as a
newspaper front page, with the date and story labels typeset over it.
`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
